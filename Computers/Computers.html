Computers

Computers are an integral part of daily life since they were invented in the late 1890's by Charles Babbage.
It consists of a Computer Tower, a keyboard, a mouse, and a monitor. A computer can vary in price.
There are many components of a Computer. The most common inside tower components are:
  1. CPU (Central Processing Unit)
  2. Motherboard
  3. RAM
  4. Storage
  5. Cooling
  6. Power Supply
  7. Optional:
    • GPU
The most common peripherals are:
  1. Keyboard
  2. Mouse
  3. Monitor
  4. Optional:
    • Headset
    • Webcam
    • Mouse Pad
    • Microphone
